FY2025 Conference Impact Reports

Technical reports and industry insights gathered from foundation-sponsored conference attendances.

NSDI 2025: Networked Systems Design & Implementation
Author: Ajay Anubolu | Date: 04/30/2025 | Location: Chapel Hill, NC

Overview: NSDI 2025 brought together researchers and practitioners to discuss advances in distributed systems, networking, and large-scale machine learning infrastructure. The clear theme was convergence: networking, hardware, and AI are increasingly interdependent.

1. Infrastructure for Machine Learning

AutoCCL

Automates distributed training communication optimization, achieving 1.23× bandwidth improvements. Core insight: configurations must adapt to workloads.

SuperServe

A dynamic ML inference serving framework featuring SubnetAct for instantaneous model switching and reactive scheduling.

2. Machine Learning for Networks

Mutant: A reinforcement learning-based congestion control framework that learns from a pool of CC protocols without large datasets.

3. RDMA and Network Transport

Sessions highlighted scalability issues in RDMA networks (ScalaCN). Solutions included Juneberry (redesigning RPC for ultra-low latency) and ODRP (event-based RDMA offloading).

4. Operational Experiences

AliCCS (Alibaba): An ML-driven congestion control system.
Aegis: Fault detection for large-scale AI training, notably identifying fiber link reliability issues.

GTC 2025: NVIDIA GPU Technology Conference
Author: Ajay Anubolu | Date: 03/19/2025 | Focus: AI, Robotics, Edge Compute

Overview: GTC 2025 highlighted the vision of end-to-end accelerated computing. Key innovations centered on robotics validation (Isaac Sim), agentic AI systems, and efficient GPU-based preprocessing pipelines.

1. Edge AI and Robotics

The "Edge-to-Cloud AI Lifecycle" was central. Models are trained in data centers, verified in simulation (Isaac Platform), and deployed for inference, minimizing the "sim-to-real" gap.

2. Autonomous Vehicles (AV 2.0)

Next-gen AVs leverage Cosmos World for reasoning. The AV 2.0 stack emphasizes "Agentic Autonomy"—systems capable of real-time reasoning from first and third-person perspectives.

3. Vision and Search Acceleration

Tools like nvImageCodec and cv-cuda eliminate CPU bottlenecks, moving preprocessing entirely to the GPU to improve throughput for semantic visual search.

OFC 2025: Optical Fiber Communication Conference
Author: Ajay Anubolu | Date: 04/03/2025 | Focus: Optical Networking

Overview: OFC 2025 showcased the physical infrastructure redefining high-speed interconnects for AI data centers, with a focus on 800G and 1.6T Ethernet technologies.

1. Emerging Trends

  • 800G & 1.6T Transceivers: PAM4-based modules enabling next-gen backbones.
  • Liquid-Cooled Optical Switches: Supermicro demonstrated chassis for 51.2 Tbps ASICs.

2. Optical Switches and Interconnects

Technologies included Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) to reduce electrical trace lengths and Silicon Photonics Switches for near-zero latency.

3. Cable Technologies

Analyzed distinctions between Single-Mode (long-haul), Multi-Mode (short-reach), and Active Optical Cables (AOCs) for plug-and-play 400G/800G connections.
